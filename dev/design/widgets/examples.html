Mock examples for the widgets / diagram-as-UI arc. Settled model: a panel IS a node (1×1 = today's node); its interior is a per-cell 26px socket grid; content sits in merged socket regions — text (optional outline) or a glyph — with configurable alignment. label == text; an input/button = text + outline; a form is ONE panel with content regions (no child hulls). No recursive nesting. Existing entities render via the real kernel; dotted boxes are the 26px sockets.
