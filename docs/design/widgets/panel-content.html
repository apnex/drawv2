Panel content — socket groups vs interior-edge content

The same form (a panel with a title + two inputs) rendered two ways, to settle how widgets sit INSIDE a panel before we build. Model so far: hull → per-cell 26px sockets → content (border may sit ON the socket border, never beyond / never the hull).

Scenario 1 — socket groups: each interior widget is a child node/panel with its own frame (a "socket group" around its sockets). → nested frames: form hull → child hull → child sockets → content. The extra child frame is the green box you flagged.
Scenario 2 — no socket groups: widgets are frameless content placed directly in the form's sockets; the input/text outline sits on the interior socket edges, so the form's outer margin is untouched. → one frame: form hull → form sockets → content outline.
